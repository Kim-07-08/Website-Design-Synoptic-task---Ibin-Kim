Our Courses

Advanced Diploma in Art & Design

This program provides students with a comprehensive understanding of art and design principles, techniques, and practices. Students will explore various mediums and develop their creative skills through hands-on projects and collaborative work.

Advanced Diploma in Digital Design

This program focuses on the intersection of art, design, and technology. Students will learn to create digital content, including animations, and interactive media. The curriculum emphasizes both technical proficiency and creative expression, preparing students for careers in digital design and related fields.

Advanced Diploma in Photography

This program offers students the opportunity to develop their skills in photography, including technical aspects, composition, and post-processing techniques. Students will gain hands-on experience in various photography styles and genres, preparing them for professional careers in photography and related industries.
