How to Apply :

To apply for admission to our programs, please follow these steps:

  1. Visit our official website and navigate to the "Admission" section.
  2. Complete the online application form with your personal and academic information.
  3. Submit the required documents, including your academic transcripts and any other supporting materials.
  4. Wait for our review and decision notification.

Admission Requirements :

The following are the general admission requirements for our programs:

  • Completion of secondary education or equivalent.
  • Submission of a portfolio (depending on the program).
  • Passing of an entrance examination (if applicable).
