About Us

We are the MCAST Institute for the Creative Arts (ICA), located primarily in Mosta, Malta. We offer vocational education, from introductory certificates to bachelor's and master's degrees, covering fine arts, design, photography, media, game art, and performing arts. Serves as the main hub for MCAST’s art and design programs ranging from foundational levels up to undergraduate and postgraduate degrees. Includes professional photo and video studios, ceramic workshops with kilns, and dedicated performance/dance spaces. Regularly hosts public exhibitions, student showcases, and festivals like the annual ICA Festival and the cultural KampuSajf event series.
